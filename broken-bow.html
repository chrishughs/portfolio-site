Here's a quick tour of a Shopify Plus rebuild I did for Broken Bow, a western heritage apparel brand.

Their store was running on an outdated version of the Impulse theme and desperately needed a refresh, so I updated it to the latest version and redesigned every page. On the product page, I swapped the image slideshow for an image grid, moved the price into the add-to-cart button, and added a sticky add-to-cart bar that appears as you scroll.

The result? Conversion rate rose by over 20% after launch, with more shoppers adding to cart and reaching checkout.
